What CineCinch is

CineCinch is a Windows app by a solo developer that answers a question most video tools skip: which files are actually wasting your disk space? It scans a folder or drive and ranks every video by megabytes per second of playback, so the true space hogs surface instantly. Then it batch-compresses just those files to H.265, with quality adapted per file, and it keeps an output only if it is genuinely smaller than the original.

Fact sheet

  • Platform: Windows 10/11 (64-bit), distributed through the Microsoft Store
  • Price model: pay once, no subscription; free 7-day trial with unlimited scanning and compression capped at 10 files
  • Privacy: 100 percent local processing; no cloud upload, no account, no telemetry
  • Encoding: CPU-only x265 (H.265/HEVC), a deliberate quality-per-byte choice; no GPU/NVENC encoding
  • Diagnosis: ranks every video by MB per second of playback before anything is compressed
  • Safety: outputs are kept only if smaller; originals are deleted only on verified success when the user opts in
  • Independent review: Softpedia editorial review, 4.5/5
  • Developer: Chad Crawford, solo developer

Story angles that fit real coverage

  • The diagnosis gap: HandBrake, Tdarr, and every converter assume you already know which files to compress. Ranking a library by MB per second is the missing first step, and it regularly shows that a handful of files hold most of the reclaimable space.
  • The no-Docker option: media-server audiences are told to run Tdarr or Unmanic; CineCinch is the answer for readers who found containers, nodes, and flows to be overkill for one full drive.
  • Local-only in an upload-everything category: most "compress a video" advice sends people to websites with upload caps; family videos never leave the machine here.
