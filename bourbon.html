🛍️ Bottle shopping: distillery exclusives

Every stop sells bottles that never reach stores (remember: checked luggage only):

· Michter's: Fort Nelson Reserve barrel-strength bourbon is the on-site exclusive; Toasted releases sometimes hit the shelf. Worth asking what is in stock the day of.

· Angel's Envy: the Distillery Series (a two-grain bourbon and a peated-cask rye among them) is sold only at the distillery.

· Rabbit Hole: Distillery Series exclusives such as Starlino (vermouth-barrel bourbon) and Amrule (maple-brule rye) in the NuLu shop.

· Bardstown Bourbon Co.: Distillery Reserve Series, including Cathedral French Oak, a 19-year bourbon finished on oak tied to the Notre-Dame restoration.

· Evan Williams: the Square 6 series (high-rye, wheated, rye) is distilled on site at the Experience and sold only there.

· Peerless: store-pick single barrels in the gift shop.

· Buzzard's Roost: the bottle shop is already the planned Monday stop.
